3 · Druggability assessment Module 3 · report PDF · folder ↗

Which of my 15 genes look like proteins a drug could act on?

Method

I used protein language model embeddings to compare my 15 proteins with proteins that already have drugs, giving each a small-molecule score (p_sm) and an antibody score (p_ab), and re-ranked the list.

4 · Structural assessment Module 4 · report PDF · folder ↗

Do my two lead targets have a place where a drug could bind?

Method

For ITGAX and APLNR I read AlphaFold's confidence (pLDDT), compared each prediction with a structure solved in the lab, and searched for pockets with fpocket.
