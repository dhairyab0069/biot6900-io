Summary of findings

  1. Combining brain RNA, brain protein and GWAS gave 1,115 genes with evidence in all three; known Alzheimer's genes ranked near the top (APOE 11th).
  2. Most of my top 15 suit antibodies; APLNR is the one clear small-molecule candidate.
  3. APLNR's predicted structure has a confident pocket where a real drug binds; ITGAX has none, so an antibody is its realistic route.
  4. Final shortlist: APLNR, ITGAX, PECAM1, HLA-DRB1, APOE.
  5. For CYP2C19, variant scores placed known broken alleles in a benign band, so no uncertain variant was reclassified.
  6. A published Alzheimer's risk score works best in Europeans (AUC 0.672) and is at chance in the African-ancestry group (0.493) in the course's simulated data.

Can variant-effect scores classify CYP2C19's uncertain variants?

Method

I fetched ClinVar labels with CADD, REVEL, AlphaMissense and gnomAD frequencies, calibrated CADD against known labels, and tried to reclassify the variants of uncertain significance (VUS).

7 · Polygenic risk across ancestries Module 6 · report PDF · folder ↗

Important: disease status in this section was simulated by the course, because 1000 Genomes has no medical records. The results illustrate the problem; they do not measure real Alzheimer's risk.

Does an Alzheimer's risk score work equally well across ancestries?

Method

I applied a published 22-SNP Alzheimer's score (PGS000334), which includes APOE, to 2,336 real genomes from five ancestry groups, then measured how well it separates cases from controls in each group.

Results

AncestrynAUCAUC without APOEAbove pooled top-10% cutoff
European (EUR)5000.6720.5887.8%
South Asian (SAS)4890.6110.5824.3%

Reference: AUC 0.5 is chance. The pooled cutoff flags 10% of everyone combined. Cross-validated AUC: age alone 0.562, age plus score 0.618.

Interpretation: the score works best in the group it was built in. Without APOE every group sits near 0.58, so the drop in the African-ancestry group comes from how the course data simulated APOE's effect, not from APOE biology. One cutoff for everyone would flag 28% of one group and 2% of another.

8 · Limitations

  • Sections 2 to 4 use gene-level summary data, so nothing here is per patient.
  • Only ITGAX and APLNR were assessed structurally; PECAM1, HLA-DRB1 and APOE have no structure evidence.
  • Druggability scores reflect resemblance to past drug targets, not proof that a drug will work.
  • Section 7's disease labels are simulated; its AUCs are illustrations.

Full methods, data sources and what didn't work are in each module's report and README in the main repository.
